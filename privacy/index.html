Plain language

Your privacy

This page was last updated on November 17, 2026.

This page explains, in plain words, what information The Wisdom Edge collects and how we use it. We keep it simple on purpose.

This is not legal advice. It is a clear description of our practices.

Who we are

The Wisdom Edge is an education brand that helps adults 55 and older use AI at a comfortable pace, save life stories, explore family history, and stay safer from scams.

I am Glenn Lee, and I write to you from this site. Our website is thewisdomedge.com. The best way to reach me is support@thewisdomedge.com.

What we collect

When you sign up on our website, we collect:

  • Your email address, so we can send you the free AI Scam Shield Guide and our weekly tips.
  • Your first name, if you share it, so we can greet you in a friendly way.

We collect this through the sign-up form on our website. That form is run by our email service, Kit.

If you email us, we will also have whatever you choose to write in that message, along with your email address.

We do not ask for your Social Security number, bank details, or passwords.

Why we collect it

We use your email, and your first name if you shared it, to:

  • Send you the free AI Scam Shield Guide.
  • Send you one short, useful AI tip each week, and occasional notes about The Wisdom Edge.
  • Reply if you write to us.
  • If you later buy a course or other product, deliver what you paid for and send receipts or help related to that purchase.

We do not use your information for any other purpose.

Our email service, Kit

We use a company called Kit to send email and to keep our mailing list. When you sign up, Kit stores your email address and your first name, if you shared it.

Kit has its own privacy policy, which you can read at kit.com/privacy.

You confirm your email first

We use double opt-in. That means after you sign up, we send you a short confirmation email. Your address is not added to the weekly list until you click the confirm button in that email.

This helps make sure nobody can sign you up with an address that is not yours.

You can unsubscribe at any time

Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link. You can click it at any time, and we will stop sending the weekly tips.

You can also email support@thewisdomedge.com and ask us to remove you. We will do that promptly.

We never sell your information

We never sell your name, your email, or any other information you share with us. Not now, not ever.

We do not share your information with other companies for their marketing. We share it only with the services that help us run The Wisdom Edge, such as Kit, and only as needed to send your email or complete a purchase.

Cookies and analytics

A cookie is a small file that a website may store on your computer or phone to help the site work.

Our website may use simple cookies that help pages load and forms work. We do not currently use advertising trackers, and we do not currently use a Meta Pixel.

If we run ads later, we may add a Meta Pixel. That is a small tool from Meta, the company that runs Facebook, that helps us see whether our ads are useful. It does not tell us your name. If we add it, we will update this page.

If you buy something from us

When you buy a course or another product, the purchase is handled through Kit Commerce, which is part of Kit.

Kit and its payment partner process the payment. We do not store your full card number.

We will use your name and email to send what you bought, and to help if you have a question about your order.

How to reach us

If you have a question about your information, or want us to correct or delete it, email support@thewisdomedge.com.

Please allow a little time for us to reply. We read every message.

Changes to this page

If our practices change, we will update this page and change the date at the top.

This page is for your information. It is not legal, financial, or medical advice.
