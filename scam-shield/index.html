About Glenn Lee

As a retired general contractor, I thought I had a pretty good eye for what’s real and what’s not. But I’ll be honest with you: in the last few years, it has become harder and harder to tell a real photo, voice, or message from an AI fake. Sometimes it’s impossible.

That’s why I started The Wisdom Edge. I wanted to build a simple system to help us stand strong in this brave new AI world, and to give real value back to the generation that paved the way for the rest of us.

This guide gathers advice from official sources, like the FBI and the FTC, and lists every source at the back so you can check it yourself. It is for learning. If money has already been lost, call your bank first.
